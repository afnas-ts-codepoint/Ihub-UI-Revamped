- Preserves six categories, seven activities, twenty sub-activities, exact codes/names/allocations/commitments/forecasts, category/activity expansion, editable leaf cells, add/delete line, hide/remove category, hidden-category restore, expand/collapse, and reset.

- `src/features/budgeting/domain/__fixtures__/budget-sheet.csv` is the exact golden output.
