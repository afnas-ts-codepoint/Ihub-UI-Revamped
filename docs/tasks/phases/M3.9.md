1. **D12:** keep standalone `/reports` and future `/home/reports` as separate views. M3.9 changes only `/reports`.
2. **FilterForm ownership:** `ReportsScreen` is its only actual prototype consumer and production had no consumer. It remains local to `features/reports`; no speculative `shared/filter` promotion was made.
3. **Catalogue count:** standalone `ReportsScreen` contains 18 reports, including `Revenue Projection`, although the phase plan and literal subtitle source say 17. All 18 were adopted; the literal source text remains unchanged in the locale resources and is not rendered because the prototype `PageHeader` suppresses its `sub` prop.
4. **Arabic:** standalone report/group/filter/placeholder labels remain English in Arabic mode, while the header is translated and layout direction is RTL. The separate bilingual Home/navigation catalogue was not substituted.
5. **Heading parity follow-up:** visual and source re-review confirmed that the prototype `PageHeader` suppresses its `sub` prop through `false && sub`. The visible subtitle was removed, the literal resource was retained, and the temporary `M3.9-H1` deviation was withdrawn.
